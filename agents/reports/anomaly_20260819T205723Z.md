# Elevated player bust rate for aggressive_20 vs standard_17 pairing

- **Pairing:** aggressive_20 vs standard_17
- **Severity:** low
- **Detected:** 20260819T205723Z

## Evidence
Over 50 rounds: win=30.0%, loss=66.0%, push=4.0%, bust=62.0%. Player bust rate of 62.0% breaches the configured ceiling of 40%. The loss rate (66%) is only slightly higher than the bust rate (62%), consistent with most losses being bust-driven auto-losses via Table._outcome triggered by player_hand.is_bust().

## Likely cause
The flagged behavior is expected and by design, not a bug. AggressivePlayerStrategy (name="aggressive_20") in casino/strategies.py hits on any hand value below 20, meaning it will keep drawing cards on hands like 17, 18, or 19 that a normal player would stand on. Since drawing an additional card on a hand already in the high-teens has a high probability of pushing the total over 21 (per Hand.value()/is_bust() in casino/hand.py), this strategy inherently produces a much higher bust rate than the "hit until 17" baseline. The docstring explicitly notes it is deliberately over-aggressive and kept in the strategy library as a stress-test fixture specifically to give the anomaly monitor real signal to react to. The Table.play_round logic itself is correct (bust check happens right after the player's hit loop, and it evaluates the dealer only if the player hasn't busted), so the mechanics are functioning as intended.

## Recommendation
No code fix is required for a defect since this is the intended stress-test behavior of AggressivePlayerStrategy. Confirm with the team whether the aggressive_20 vs standard_17 pairing should be excluded from production anomaly alerting, or whether the bust-rate ceiling should be made strategy-aware rather than a single global 40 percent threshold. If this pairing exists solely to validate the monitor, tag it as such in configuration so future alerts on it are suppressed or downgraded.
