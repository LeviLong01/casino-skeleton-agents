# Elevated player bust rate / depressed win rate for basic_17 vs standard_17 pairing

- **Pairing:** basic_17 vs standard_17
- **Severity:** medium
- **Detected:** 20260820T020838Z

## Evidence
Over the last 50 rounds: player bust rate 42.0% breached the 40% ceiling; player win rate 28.0% fell below the 30%-55% expected range. Loss rate (56%) and bust rate (42%) are closely aligned, consistent with busts being the dominant loss mechanism, and the strategy source shows should_hit() never inspects dealer_upcard, meaning play decisions are made purely on the player's own total with no adaptation to the dealer's shown card.

## Likely cause
BasicPlayerStrategy.should_hit() in casino/strategies.py implements a naive "hit until 17" rule that completely ignores the dealer_upcard parameter (and hand composition, e.g. soft vs hard totals). Because the player strategy always draws whenever hand.value() < 17 regardless of what the dealer shows, it will keep hitting on hands (e.g. hard 12-16) in situations where a real basic-strategy chart would stand (for example against a weak dealer upcard) or otherwise play differently. This structurally biases the player toward drawing more cards than an actual basic-strategy player would, which mechanically increases the probability of busting (is_bust() triggers immediately when value()>21) and, since Table.play_round() awards the round to the dealer on any player bust, directly suppresses the win rate and inflates the loss rate. The dealer strategy (standard_17) is a correct, standard implementation, so the asymmetry is isolated to the player-side strategy's oversimplified decision rule.

## Recommendation
1) Increase the sample size beyond 50 rounds before treating this as a confirmed regression, since bust/win rates at n=50 have wide confidence intervals. 2) Independently re-run basic_17 vs standard_17 for several thousand rounds to establish a statistically stable baseline bust/win rate for this specific (deliberately simplistic) strategy, and compare against the monitor's configured thresholds (40% bust ceiling, 30-55% win range) — those thresholds may have been calibrated for a true basic-strategy player, not this naive always-hit-to-17 implementation. 3) If the intent is for basic_17 to approximate real basic strategy, update BasicPlayerStrategy.should_hit() to consider dealer_upcard (and soft/hard hand distinction) per a standard basic-strategy chart; otherwise, recalibrate the monitor's expected ranges specifically for the basic_17/standard_17 pairing to avoid false-positive anomaly flags.
