# Elevated player bust rate for aggressive_20 vs standard_17 pairing

- **Pairing:** aggressive_20 vs standard_17
- **Severity:** low
- **Detected:** 20260820T024627Z

## Evidence
Over the last 50 rounds of aggressive_20 vs standard_17: win=32.0%, loss=66.0%, push=2.0%, bust=64.0%, breaching the configured player bust rate ceiling of 40%. Source inspection confirms AggressivePlayerStrategy.should_hit returns True for any hand value < 20, whereas the dealer (StandardDealerStrategy) and the more conservative BasicPlayerStrategy both stop hitting at 17, meaning aggressive_20 draws far more cards per round and therefore busts far more often — consistent with the observed 64% bust rate.

## Likely cause
The player bust rate is not an anomaly in the sense of a bug — it is the expected statistical consequence of the AggressivePlayerStrategy (name="aggressive_20") in casino/strategies.py, which overrides should_hit to keep drawing cards until the hand value reaches 20 (`return hand.value() < 20`) instead of stopping at 17 like BasicPlayerStrategy. Because the player hits on any hand totaling 17, 18, or 19 (in addition to lower totals), the probability of drawing a card that pushes the hand over 21 is much higher than under the standard 17-stop rule. The class docstring even states it is "Deliberately over-aggressive: kept in the strategy library as a stress-test fixture so the anomaly monitor has real, occasional signal to react to," confirming this is intentional test fixture behavior rather than a defect in Table.play_round, Hand.value/is_bust, or the dealer logic.

## Recommendation
Confirm with the team whether aggressive_20 is intentionally deployed in this table configuration as a stress-test fixture (per its docstring) or was mistakenly enabled in a live/monitored pairing. If it's the former, suppress/allowlist this specific strategy pairing in the anomaly monitor (or raise its bust-rate ceiling) so it doesn't generate false-positive alerts. If it was enabled by mistake, replace it with BasicPlayerStrategy (name="basic_17") for this table and re-run the monitor to confirm bust rate returns below the 40% ceiling.
