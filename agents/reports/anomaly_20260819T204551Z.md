# Elevated player bust rate for aggressive_20 vs standard_17 pairing

- **Pairing:** aggressive_20 vs standard_17
- **Severity:** low
- **Detected:** 20260819T204551Z

## Evidence
Over 50 rounds: win=34.0%, loss=64.0%, push=2.0%, bust=64.0%. The bust rate of 64.0% breaches the configured ceiling of 40%. Note bust=64.0% equals loss=64.0%, consistent with Table.play_round() immediately awarding the round to the dealer whenever player_hand.is_bust() is True (dealer play/comparison never occurs in those cases).

## Likely cause
This is expected, deliberate behavior rather than a bug. AggressivePlayerStrategy (name="aggressive_20") in casino/strategies.py hits until hand.value() >= 20, versus the basic strategy's threshold of 17. Since hand values in the mid-teens have a substantial chance of busting on a single hit, and this strategy forces extra hits at 17, 18, and 19 where a standard strategy would stand, it structurally accumulates far more bust risk per hand. The docstring explicitly states this strategy is "deliberately over-aggressive... kept in the strategy library as a stress-test fixture so the anomaly monitor has real, occasional signal to react to," meaning it was designed to trip exactly this kind of alert.

## Recommendation
Confirm with the team whether aggressive_20 is intentionally exposed to live matchmaking or should remain confined to test/stress-test fixtures. If it should not face live thresholds, exclude aggressive_20 from the anomaly monitor's live strategy pool or tag it as a known high-bust-rate strategy to prevent recurring false-positive incidents. If it must stay monitored, either raise the bust-rate ceiling for this specific pairing or add an allowlist/annotation in the monitor config referencing the stress-test-fixture docstring in casino/strategies.py.
