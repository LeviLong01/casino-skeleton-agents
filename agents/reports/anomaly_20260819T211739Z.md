# Elevated Player Bust Rate for aggressive_20 vs standard_17 Pairing

- **Pairing:** aggressive_20 vs standard_17
- **Severity:** low
- **Detected:** 20260819T211739Z

## Evidence
Over 50 rounds: bust=70.0% (ceiling 40%), win=22.0% (expected range 30-55%), loss=70.0%, push=8.0%. The aggressive_20 strategy hits until hand.value() >= 20, continuing to draw in the 17-19 range where bust risk is high, directly explaining the elevated bust rate and correspondingly depressed win rate versus the monitor's baseline thresholds (which likely reflect basic_17-style play).

## Likely cause
This is expected, deliberate behavior rather than a bug. AggressivePlayerStrategy.should_hit() in casino/strategies.py hits on any hand value < 20, meaning the player keeps drawing cards through the 17-19 range where standard strategy would stop. Since roughly 12 of the 13 card ranks (4-10, J, Q, K, and effectively any additional card) can push a hand from the high teens over 21, continuing to hit at 17-19 sharply increases bust probability compared to stopping at 17. The docstring explicitly notes this strategy is "kept in the strategy library as a stress-test fixture so the anomaly monitor has real, occasional signal to react to" — confirming the high bust/loss rate is the intended, designed outcome of pairing this strategy against a standard dealer, not a code defect in Table, Hand, or Deck logic.

## Recommendation
Confirm with the monitor/config owner whether aggressive_20 is intentionally excluded from normal anomaly alerting (since it's documented as a stress-test fixture). If so, add an allowlist/exception in the anomaly monitor for the aggressive_20 vs standard_17 pairing (or any pairing involving aggressive_20) so it doesn't page as a false-positive incident. If the monitor should still track it, adjust its expected win/bust thresholds specifically for aggressive_20 to reflect its known higher bust rate rather than the standard 40% ceiling.</recommendation>
