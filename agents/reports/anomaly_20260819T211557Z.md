The most probable explanation is ordinary sampling variance from a small round count, amplified by a static expected-range threshold (30%-55%) that is not wide enough to account for the variance inherent at n=50. A secondary, unverified possibility is bias in the Deck/Card shuffling implementation (not shown in the provided source), which was not available for review and would need separate inspection if the effect persists at scale.

Concurrently, widen or make the monitor's expected-range thresholds sample-size-aware (e.g., compute a confidence interval scaled to n instead of a fixed 30%-55% band) to reduce false alerts at low round counts.
